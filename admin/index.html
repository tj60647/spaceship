01 / controls

Shared demo spending limit

Shared by everyone, including the owner. Covers text generation and decision-model calls; signing in does not create a personal balance.

Set the total cap, not an amount to add. A $10 limit with $2 used/reserved leaves $8 shared.

How the spending limit works

Saving does not buy credits or charge the full amount. The owner can raise the limit here (up to $100), but has no bypass. A $0 limit blocks paid calls. The cap does not reset automatically.

Used/reserved includes in-flight requests and conservative estimates when a provider did not report cost. OpenRouter credit and provider-side limits still apply.

After raising the limit or resuming calls, return to the flight deck and choose Retry / new story.
